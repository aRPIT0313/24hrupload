SHORT QUALITY SYSTEM
====================

The adaptive Shorts pipeline now optimizes for scroll-stop + subscribers, not generic facts.

1. TOPIC QUALITY GATE
- Gemini creates a large candidate pool before writing scripts.
- Candidates are scored for hook strength, broad appeal, visual strength, surprise,
  shareability and subscriber potential.
- Candidates with weak hooks, narrow appeal or high technicality are rejected.
- Duplicate underlying subjects are rejected, not just duplicate titles.
- Fresh trend candidates are mixed with evergreen candidates.

2. HOOK ENGINE
- Every Short gets 3 hook options.
- Gemini selects the strongest hook.
- The selected hook is forced to be the first spoken sentence.
- The first visual is forced to display the selected hook.
- The first scene is ~2 seconds rather than an equal-duration scene.

3. STORY STRUCTURE
- 0-2 sec: scroll-stop hook
- 2-6 sec: setup
- 6-12 sec: escalation
- 12-18 sec: reveal/payoff
- 18-23 sec: satisfying final detail
- 23-26 sec: curiosity CTA

4. SUBSCRIBER CTA
- Every Short receives a topic-specific curiosity CTA.
- Gemini generates the CTA separately for each Short.
- Generic repeated CTAs are prohibited.
- CTA is part of the spoken voiceover and comes after the payoff.

5. ADAPTIVE LEARNING
The system tracks recent Shorts and learns:
- comparable/engaged performance
- retention when Analytics is available
- subscribers per 1,000 comparable views
- category performance
- hook-style performance
- title-word signals
- publish-hour performance
- weekday performance

Older Shorts without saved metadata receive coarse category/hook labels inferred from titles,
so existing channel history can still contribute to learning.

6. PUBLISHING
- AI mode: automatically chooses historically stronger windows while exploring nearby windows.
- Manual mode: user can specify the exact date/time for every Short.

The system does not guarantee a particular number of views or subscribers. It is designed to
continuously improve the probability of strong cold-viewer retention and subscriber conversion.
